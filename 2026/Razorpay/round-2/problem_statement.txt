HTTP Load Balancer
Your job is to build a simple http load balancer that reads a set of backend http URLS and is able to respond to the client queries. Your service will bootstrap itself with a list of backend IP addresses for server instances, do constant health checks on them and ensure that the backend services are up and active. In case of a backend failure, the load balancer will remove that server from serving active traffic.
Kind of load balancing should be configurable at a per endpoint level.

Examples
/api/v1/users , round robin to IPs from service A
/api/v1/payments , <some other LB method> to IPs from service B
