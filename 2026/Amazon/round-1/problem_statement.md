**Optimal Coin Pick Strategy**

Pick Up Coins for Maximum Gain. An even number of coins is arranged in a line.
Two players, Player A and Player B, take turns selecting one coin at a time. On each turn, a player may choose only one of the two coins at either end of the line.

The game ends when all coins have been selected. The player with the higher total value wins. A player may not skip a turn.

Both Player A and Player B play optimally:
- Player A tries to maximize their total coin value.
- Player B also plays strategically and tries to maximize their own total coin value, which may reduce Player A's possible total.

Design an algorithm to compute the maximum total value that Player A, the starting player, can guarantee.
